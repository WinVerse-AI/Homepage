Evidence dossier

A compact critical appraisal of the facial-FES research pathway.

2014 · Neuromodulation of emotion

Facial FES changed some emotional responses, but the primary endpoints were not significant. This is mechanistic precedent, not clinical proof.

Zariffa et al. ↗

2019 · Pilot feasibility study

Ten participants showed symptom-score improvements after ten sessions. The study was small, uncontrolled and did not establish durability or causation.

Kapadia et al. ↗

2025 · Sham-controlled protocol

A registered randomised feasibility study is designed to test the method more rigorously. A protocol is evidence of intent—not an outcome.

Gholamali Nezhad et al. ↗

What remains unproven

Safety and efficacy, dose-response, durability, sham separation, participant selection and repeatability at product scale.
