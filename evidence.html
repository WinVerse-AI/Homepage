Research and claim boundary

Clinical research continues. Consumer claims remain narrow.

UHN/KITE-origin studies investigate medical questions. WinVerse™ does not convert those studies into a consumer efficacy claim. The initial commercial pathway is non-medical and will be validated separately for safety, usability and any future substantiated wellbeing benefit.

What WinVerse™ will not claim

No diagnosis, treatment, prevention, mood-improvement, stress-reduction or other health benefit will be claimed without product-specific evidence and jurisdiction-appropriate review.

Why everyday wellbeing matters

WHO estimates that depression and anxiety account for 12 billion lost working days each year. This does not prove a benefit for SerotoniX™; it shows the scale of unmet demand for practical, accessible and evidence-conscious ways to build moments of pause into daily working life.
