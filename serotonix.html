01A · Platform / SerotoniX™

A research platform before it becomes a product claim.

SerotoniX™ brings the facial interface, stimulation workflow and evidence record into one investigational platform designed for repeatable research—not unsupervised treatment.

Current state

Research and productisation phase Pre-market · Investigational · No efficacy claim

The premise

Make the research delivery reliable enough to test the science properly.

Facial functional electrical stimulation may provide a way to study the relationship between evoked expression and emotional experience. The scientific question remains open. The enabling engineering problem is already clear: fit, placement, dose and session records must become more consistent.

Evidence boundary

SerotoniX™ is investigational. It is not approved to diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease, and this website does not claim that it is effective.

Working definition

What SerotoniX™ is—and what it is not.

01

Research interface

A participant-specific facial interface intended to support repeatable electrode positioning and documented fit.

02

Study workflow

A controlled process for configuration, supervised use, session verification and research-quality records.

03

Not a consumer wellness device

No direct-to-consumer positioning, autonomous stimulation or clinical promise is implied at this stage.

Current programme

Three questions govern the next phase.

  1. 01
    Can delivery be reproduced?

    Control fit, placement, configuration and session records across participants and sites.

  2. 02
    Can the evidence survive better controls?

    Separate feasibility and plausibility from verified clinical effect.

  3. 03
    Can the architecture scale responsibly?

    Move beyond labour-intensive one-off fabrication without weakening safety or traceability.
