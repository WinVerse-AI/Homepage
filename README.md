# WinVerse™ Website

Responsive, multilingual static website for WinVerse™ and SerotoniX™, its first consumer wellbeing product programme.

The interface supports English, Chinese, Japanese, French, German and Spanish. Language preference is retained locally in the visitor's browser.

## Commercialisation position

WinVerse™ is advancing facial-stimulation technology originating at UHN/KITE through an authorised commercialisation pathway. The underlying authority is intentionally described without publishing the private inter-company authorisation chain.

The programme follows two clearly separated tracks:

1. **Continuing research:** UHN/KITE research teams continue to investigate clinical questions and generate scientific evidence.
2. **Consumer commercialisation:** WinVerse™ develops a non-medical consumer wellbeing product, beginning with guided facial-activation and relaxation routines for everyday use.

The first route to market is B2C. Product engineering, safety, usability, manufacturing, supply chain and consumer distribution are led by WinVerse™.

## Claims policy

- The initial commercial positioning is consumer wellbeing, not medical treatment.
- The website makes no diagnosis, treatment, cure or prevention claim.
- It does not claim stress reduction, mood improvement or another health benefit without product-specific substantiation.
- Clinical research about the underlying technology is not presented as proof of consumer-product efficacy.
- Final classification, labelling and permitted claims remain subject to product design, risk assessment, testing and jurisdiction-specific regulatory review.

## Site structure

- `index.html` — authorised pathway and consumer-market proposition
- `platform.html` — separation of research and commercialisation pathways
- `serotonix.html` — non-medical consumer wellbeing product direction
- `evidence.html` — published research, policy sources and claims boundary
- `company.html` — WinVerse™ commercialisation mandate
- `about.html` — leadership and responsible commercialisation roles
- `contact.html` — consumer-product, manufacturing, pilot and investment enquiries

The primary navigation opens each subject as a separate page rather than scrolling through one long page. SerotoniX™ appears within the Platform navigation group. Desktop layouts use a compact executive-summary format; tablet and mobile pages reflow naturally for readability.

The contact form uses a `mailto:` hand-off so visitors review and send their message through their own email application. No form data is stored by the static site.
